Professional Profile

Biomedical & Electrical Engineering
I build and integrate engineering systems for biomedical research, medical devices and experimental platforms.

My background combines biomedical engineering, electronics, embedded systems, instrumentation, biomedical optics, robotics, FPGA and experimental validation. I am especially motivated by problems where hardware, software and physiology need to work as one system.

I work with an implementation-oriented mindset: define the need, translate it into measurable requirements, build the system, calibrate it, test it and iterate from data.
